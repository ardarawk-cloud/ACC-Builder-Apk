# AM STUDIO Music Distribution — Brand System v1

## Primary name
AM STUDIO

## Product descriptor
MUSIC DISTRIBUTION

## Positioning
Independent, premium, technology-led music distribution infrastructure for artists and labels.

## Logo direction
Primary symbol: compact **AM** monogram built from strong geometric strokes. The `A` carries the warm-gold accent; the `M` remains titanium-white. The symbol must remain readable at Android launcher-icon size and must not depend on tiny text.

Wordmark lockup:
- AM STUDIO
- MUSIC DISTRIBUTION as smaller descriptor when space allows.

## Color tokens
- Carbon Black: `#0A0B0F`
- Deep Panel: `#111319`
- Titanium White: `#EDEFF4`
- Warm Gold: `#F2B84B`
- Signal Blue: `#6EA8FF` (functional/status accent only)
- Success: `#46D38A`
- Risk/Error: `#FF6B72`

## UI principles
1. Premium dark canvas, not gaming-neon overload.
2. Gold is reserved for brand/primary actions, not every component.
3. Status colors communicate operations, not decoration.
4. Large tap targets and high contrast for Android usage.
5. Release artwork should be the visual focus once real catalog exists.
6. No fake DSP logos bundled without appropriate asset/licensing path; text labels are acceptable in foundation builds.

## Icon rule
Launcher icon: carbon-black rounded square with centered AM monogram; no small descriptor text.
