## Full description
BALI WEDDING DJ is a premium digital wedding concierge for couples and wedding professionals planning celebrations in Bali.

Discover wedding DJ packages, select your wedding date, submit event details, receive quotations and invoices, manage payment verification, and organize your wedding music and event timeline in one place.

Key features include:

- Premium wedding DJ packages
- Wedding date request and availability flow
- Custom wedding entertainment requests
- Booking status tracking
- Quotation and invoice management
- Deposit and payment receipt workflow
- Wedding music planner
- Event timeline planning
- Direct WhatsApp contact
- Wedding gallery and reviews

Bookings are not automatically confirmed when a request is submitted. Each request is reviewed by the Bali Wedding DJ team. A booking becomes confirmed only after availability, quotation acceptance, required deposit, and payment verification are completed.

BALI WEDDING DJ has served weddings in Bali since 2015, creating seamless musical journeys from ceremony to reception and after party.
