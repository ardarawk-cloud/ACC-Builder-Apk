This notice explains how Bali Wedding DJ, operated by PT NADMO STUDIO INDONESIA, handles information submitted through the booking platform.

Information we collect

We may collect names, email address, WhatsApp number, wedding date, venue information, event schedule, package selections, music preferences, special requests, booking status, invoice information, and payment proof submitted by the customer.

We use this information to review availability, prepare quotations and invoices, process and confirm payments, coordinate wedding services, provide booking updates, support customers, and maintain necessary business records.

Information may be processed by infrastructure, communications, or payment service providers when required to operate the booking service. We do not sell customer personal information.

Payment proof

Payment screenshots, files, or photos are used only to verify the related booking payment and maintain transaction records.

Retention and requests

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for booking operations, business records, dispute handling, and applicable legal obligations. Privacy requests can be sent to nadmostudio@gmail.com.
